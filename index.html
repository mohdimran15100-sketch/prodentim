What is ProDentim?

A chewable oral probiotic, not another mouthwash

ProDentim is a dietary supplement sold as soft, chewable tablets rather than a liquid rinse or paste. Instead of scrubbing the mouth or masking odor for a few minutes, it's designed to be dissolved slowly once a day, after brushing, so its ingredients stay in contact with the teeth, gums and tongue for longer.

Each tablet is built around a blend of bacterial strains that researchers have studied in connection with oral health, combined with inulin (a prebiotic fiber), tricalcium phosphate, malic acid and peppermint. The company positions it as a daily addition to an existing oral-care routine - brushing, flossing and dental visits - rather than a stand-alone fix.

ProDentim is manufactured in the United States in a facility that is GMP-certified and registered with the FDA. Being made in an FDA-registered facility means the facility follows the agency's manufacturing and record-keeping rules for supplements - it does not mean the FDA has approved, tested or endorsed the finished product, which is standard for dietary supplements in general.

What is an oral probiotic?

Feeding the mouth's own ecosystem

The human mouth hosts hundreds of species of bacteria, living together in what scientists call the oral microbiome. In a balanced mouth, helpful strains keep less desirable ones in check. When that balance shifts - through diet, illness, medication, aging or simply time - bacteria linked to plaque buildup, gum irritation and bad breath can gain ground.

An oral probiotic is a supplement that introduces live strains of bacteria, chosen because they have been studied for a supportive role in the mouth, directly where they're needed. The goal, according to the research this category draws on, is to help re-establish a more favorable balance rather than to sterilize the mouth the way an antiseptic mouthwash attempts to.

This is a different mechanism from brushing (mechanical removal of plaque), fluoride (enamel strengthening) or antibacterial mouthwash (broad bacterial reduction). None of these approaches replace the others - they address different parts of the same picture, which is why ProDentim is marketed as a complement to, not a substitute for, standard oral hygiene.
