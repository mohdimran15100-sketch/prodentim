Product Overview

ProDentim is a chewable oral probiotic supplement - 30 soft tablets per bottle, one tablet per day, formulated with studied bacterial strains, inulin, tricalcium phosphate, malic acid and peppermint. This guide walks through the package options, what each one includes, and how the ordering and guarantee process works, so you know what to expect before you click through to checkout.

What's Included

Every package includes the stated number of ProDentim bottles (30 tablets each). The 6-bottle "best value" package has historically also included three digital bonus guides at no extra cost: a bad-breath-focused detox guide, an at-home whitening-habits guide, and a guide to an at-home oral-care routine. Bonus inclusions are set by the seller and, like pricing, can change - the checkout page will confirm exactly what's included with your specific order before you pay.

None of the bonus guides are medical documents, and none should be read as a substitute for professional dental advice - they're framed by the seller as supplementary lifestyle content, and we'd treat them the same way: a nice-to-have addition to the core product rather than a reason on their own to choose the largest package. The core value in any package is the 30 daily tablets per bottle it contains.

Guarantee

All packages are covered by a 60-day money-back guarantee starting from your purchase date. If ProDentim doesn't work out, the standard process is to contact the seller's support team - using the contact details in your order confirmation email - within that 60-day window to request a refund of the purchase price. We'd suggest saving your confirmation email and order number as soon as you check out, since you'll likely need them for any refund request.

Is It Worth the Price?

Value in a category like this depends on what you're comparing it to. Against a bottle of mouthwash or a pack of mints, ProDentim's per-day cost (roughly $1.60 to $2.30 depending on package size) is noticeably higher - but it's a different kind of product, aimed at bacterial balance rather than short-term freshening, so a direct price comparison to mouthwash isn't quite apples-to-apples. Against other named-strain oral probiotic supplements, ProDentim's per-bottle pricing and CFU count are broadly in line with the category. The 60-day guarantee is arguably the more relevant value factor for a first-time buyer: it means the real financial risk of trying the product is limited to whatever it costs you in time, not the full purchase price, provided you request a refund within the window if it isn't for you.
