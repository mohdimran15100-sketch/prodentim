Product Overview

ProDentim is a chewable oral probiotic supplement sold in bottles of 30 soft tablets, formulated with a blend of bacterial strains (L. paracasei, L. reuteri, S. salivarius) delivering roughly 3.5 billion CFU per tablet, alongside inulin, tricalcium phosphate, malic acid and peppermint. It's positioned as a daily addition to an existing oral-hygiene routine - not a replacement for brushing, flossing or dental visits - and is manufactured in a GMP-certified, FDA-registered U.S. facility.

When comparing the three package sizes, it helps to think in terms of total cost per day of supply rather than the sticker price alone. None of this is a reason to buy more than you're prepared to use - it's simply the math behind why most repeat customers choose a multi-bottle package rather than reordering a single bottle every month.
